*Background Information*

Yamaguchi University’s AI and Data Science Education
Program is designed for working professionals. Participants may participate
in four online/on-demand modules —- Introduction, Statistics, Machine Learning,
and Digital Transformation. I presented one of the 5 lectures for Statistics.
The lecture (3 hours) was on correlation and least squares regression. I prepared
teaching materials on Google Colab Notebook and presented Python code to perform
tasks related to the lecture. The lecture was recorded so that participants
could access the materials asynchronously. The teaching materials and presentation
were provided in Japanese.
